# Product vision

USER-REPORTED: provide one platform for isolated store operations, organization billing and consumer cross-store expense visibility. Platform administrators govern plans, subscriptions, usage and authorized support. Organization owners operate multiple stores without mixing store data. Consumers use one identity across approved store relationships.

Success measures are PROPOSED: no cross-store leakage, deterministic onboarding/entitlement decisions and traceable invoice decisions. Numeric targets, commercial launch scope and scale budgets remain UNKNOWN. See [scope](scope.md) and [business rules](business-rules.md).
