FACT: complete backend identity lifecycle is composed and operational in cmd/api:
1. User registration: module-level `RegisterRequest` DTO validation returning `ValidationError` (field-level 422 `validation_failed`), bcrypt cost 12 password hashing, advisory locks, duplicate account handling, and atomic DB transactions.
2. Email verification: 30-minute expiry, single-use SHA-256 tokens, 5-attempt brute-force protection, SMTP delivery with LogMailer dev fallback, and 60-second cooldown resend.
3. Login: credential validation, constant-time dummy hash comparison for non-existent accounts, active status verification, and session creation.
4. JWT authentication: 10-minute short-lived HMAC-SHA256 access tokens with strict header, claims (iss, aud, sub, sid, jti, iat, exp), and signature verification.
5. Refresh-token lifecycle: HttpOnly cookies with SameSite=Lax and Path=/ (supporting /api/auth and direct /auth endpoints without path mismatch), atomic rotation with RFC 6749 BCP 15-second grace window in PostgreSQL (migration 000005) preventing false-positive family revocation on concurrent client/StrictMode reloads, reuse detection revoking entire family outside grace period, and 30-day fixed expiry.
6. Authenticated user context: RequireAuth middleware verifying live database session, GET /auth/me current-user profile endpoint, and tenant.Context integration with UserID.
7. Logout: revoke current session family, clear refresh cookie (Max-Age: -1), and POST /auth/logout-all to revoke all sessions across all devices.
8. PostgreSQL integration: database/sql connection pool, migrations 000001–000005 applied, unprivileged eghissab_app role with NOBYPASSRLS, and transaction-scoped RLS via set_config.
9. API integration: REST routes, handlers, services, repositories, module DTO validation (`Validate() error`), and consistent httpx error envelope.
10. Rate limiting & defense: Two-tier rate limiting with IP middleware and domain service email budget; global token-bucket rate limiter middleware (`backend/internal/middleware/ratelimit.go`) with trusted proxy resolution; migration 000006 (`identity.auth_prune_attempts`) and hourly background database maintenance pruning expired `identity.auth_attempts` rows.

Domain D: Store Operations, Customers & Invoicing (INV-001, PROD-001, STOCK-001, CUST-001): IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED:
1. Catalog & Product Management: `store.products` (`id PK, store_id FK, sku, name, price, status`) with `UNIQUE (store_id, sku)`. Supports product creation, retrieval, updates, and archival.
2. Inventory Locations & Default Provisioning: `store.inventory_locations` (`id PK, store_id FK, name, is_default`) with `UNIQUE (store_id, name)`. Database trigger `store.trg_init_store_location` automatically initializes a 'Default' location on store creation.
3. Inventory Balances: `store.inventory_balances` with composite PK `(store_id, product_id, location_id)` and quantity.
4. Transactional Stock Movements: `store.stock_movements` (`id PK, store_id, product_id, location_id, quantity_delta, reason, reference_id, actor_user_id`). Balances are strictly controlled by transactional stock movements via PostgreSQL trigger `store.trg_apply_stock_movement` on insert, preventing client-side drifting or arbitrary mutations.
5. Isolated Store Customers: `store.store_customers` with `UNIQUE (store_id, customer_number)`, isolating customers per store with auto-generated sequential identifiers (`CUST-XXXXX`) and optional linking to consumer portal profiles.
6. Invoicing Lifecycle: `invoice.invoices` (`id PK, store_id, store_customer_id, invoice_number, lifecycle_status, subtotal, discount_amount, tax_amount, total_amount, paid_amount, currency, notes, invoice_date, due_date, issued_at`).
7. Immutability & Snapshots: `invoice.invoice_items` captures immutable description, SKU, quantity, unit price, discounts, and taxes. Database triggers `invoice.trg_protect_issued_invoice_items` and `invoice.trg_protect_issued_invoice` strictly prohibit modification or deletion of line items or financial totals once an invoice has been issued.
8. Sequential Invoice Numbering: Atomically generated on issuance using store-configured preferences (`store.store_settings.invoice_preferences`), ensuring gapless sequential identifiers (e.g. `INV-000001`).
9. Automatic Stock Deduction on Issuance & Reversal on Void: On issuance, negative delta stock movements (`sale_issued`) are created for all inventory line items. When voided, positive reversing movements (`sale_returned`) are automatically recorded.
10. Payment Tracking: `invoice.invoice_payments` records cash, card, and digital payments, aggregating `paid_amount` and transitioning lifecycle status to `partially_paid` or `paid`. Overpayments are validated and rejected.
11. Accounting Adjustments (Credit/Debit Notes): `invoice.invoice_adjustments` aligns with tax, credit-note, and accounting standards without rewriting immutable issued invoice history.
12. Audit Events & Consumer Decisions: `invoice.invoice_events` audit trail logs all invoice lifecycle transitions; `invoice.invoice_decisions` enables consumer portal dispute/approval workflows.

2. Temporal Invoice Access Gate (`access_from`): `consumer.customer_links.access_from` restricts historical invoice visibility, ensuring past invoices cannot leak unless explicitly granted.
